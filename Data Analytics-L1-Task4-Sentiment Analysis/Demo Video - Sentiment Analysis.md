## Video Contents

The demo video presents:

- Project title and internship information.
- Dataset loading and initial inspection.
- Exploratory data analysis of social media sentiment data.
- Cleaning and preprocessing of noisy text data.
- Emoji demojization and contraction expansion.
- Removal of URLs and user mentions while preserving sentiment-related terms.
- Consolidation of 191 granular emotion labels into Positive, Negative, and Neutral classes.
- Sentiment class distribution and class-imbalance analysis.
- TF-IDF feature extraction using unigram and bigram features.
- Training and comparison of Logistic Regression, Linear SVC, Random Forest, and Multinomial Naive Bayes models.
- Stratified cross-validation and hyperparameter tuning.
- Model evaluation using accuracy, precision, recall, F1-score, and confusion matrix.
- Sentiment distribution visualization and word clouds.
- Single-text prediction through the command-line interface.
- Final conclusions and practical NLP insights.
